# Code of Conduct

## The standard

Be straightforward and courteous. Criticise the work, not the person. Assume the other party is
acting in good faith until the evidence says otherwise.

Unacceptable: harassment of any kind, personal or political attacks, publishing anyone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, commit messages and any other space where
someone is representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report a problem by opening an issue or contacting the maintainer directly. Reports are handled
privately. The maintainer may edit, hide or delete contributions that breach this standard, and
may block repeat offenders.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short deliberately: a document nobody reads protects nobody.
